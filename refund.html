Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025

This policy outlines the terms under which refunds and cancellations are handled for coin purchases made within the ChatMeet application.

💡 Summary: Digital coin purchases are generally non-refundable once coins have been used. However, we review genuine technical issues and payment errors on a case-by-case basis.

1. Nature of Digital Goods

Coins purchased on ChatMeet are digital credits used within the application to access premium AI conversation features. As these are digital goods that are delivered instantly upon purchase, they are generally non-refundable once credited to your account and used.

2. Coin Packs Available

  • Starter Pack: 55 coins – ₹50
  • Popular Pack: 130 coins – ₹100
  • Best Value Pack: 295 coins – ₹200

3. When Refunds Are Eligible

You may be eligible for a refund in the following cases:

  • Double charge / duplicate payment: Your account was charged more than once for the same transaction.
  • Payment deducted but coins not credited: Your payment was successful but coins were not added to your account within 24 hours.
  • Technical error: A verifiable technical fault on our end caused an incorrect transaction.
  • Unauthorised transaction: A payment was made without your knowledge or consent (subject to verification).

4. When Refunds Are Not Eligible

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Coins have already been used within the app
  • Change of mind after a successful purchase
  • Dissatisfaction with AI-generated conversation content
  • Account suspension or termination due to violation of our Terms & Conditions
  • Requests made after 7 days from the date of purchase

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email us at chatmeet.india@gmail.com within 7 days of the transaction with the following details:

  • Your registered email address or mobile number
  • Date and amount of the transaction
  • Payment reference / transaction ID
  • Reason for the refund request
  • Screenshot of the payment confirmation (if available)

6. Refund Processing Time

Once your refund request is approved:

  • Refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days
  • The amount will be credited back to the original payment method
  • Bank processing times may vary (typically 3–5 additional business days)

7. Cancellation Policy

ChatMeet does not offer subscription plans at this time. All purchases are one-time coin pack purchases. There is no recurring billing or subscription to cancel.

If you wish to close your ChatMeet account, please contact us at chatmeet.india@gmail.com. Any unused coins remaining in your account at the time of closure are non-refundable.

8. Contact for Refund Queries

  • Email: chatmeet.india@gmail.com
  • Response time: Within 2 business days
  • Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
