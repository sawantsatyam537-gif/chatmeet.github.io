Last updated: 1 January 2025

ChatMeet ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ChatMeet (the "App"). Please read this policy carefully.

We may collect the following information when you use ChatMeet:

  • Account Information: Mobile number or email address used for Firebase Authentication.

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and app version.
  • Transaction Data: Records of coin purchases made within the app, including amounts and timestamps.

We use the information we collect to:

  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraudulent activity

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure, which is compliant with industry-standard security practices. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Chat data is stored on secure cloud servers. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage; payment processors for coin transactions.

5. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:
