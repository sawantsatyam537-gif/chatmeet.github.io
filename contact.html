Frequently Asked Questions

My coins were not credited after payment. What do I do?

Please wait up to 24 hours. If coins are still not credited, email us at chatmeet.india@gmail.com with your transaction ID and we'll resolve it within 2 business days.

Can I get a refund on my coin purchase?

Refunds are available for duplicate charges, technical errors, or payment deducted without coin credit. Please see our Refund Policy for full details.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at chatmeet.india@gmail.com with your registered email or mobile number and request account deletion. We'll process it within 5 business days.

Is ChatMeet available on iOS?

ChatMeet is currently available on Android via Google Play Store only. iOS support may be added in the future.

What is the minimum age to use ChatMeet?

ChatMeet is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older.
